# 18 clubs, all five throwing line doubles from the right hand, and the ZIP
# TRAVELS: it goes round the circle E -> A -> B -> C -> D -> E -> ... so nobody
# is stuck with it.
#
# Everyone throws the same ten-beat sequence, just starting it at a different
# point. Written as pass/self pairs, your five selfs are:
#
#     high self, ZIP, self, self, self
#
# and your neighbour starts that one pair later. The zip ripples round the star
# one person per beat.
#
#
# WHY THE HIGH SELF IS THERE
#
# A zip is a 1 and a plain self is a 3, so dropping a self to a zip takes a club
# out of your hands two beats early and leaves a hole where it should have
# landed. The 5 immediately before it plugs that hole: a 5 and a 1 together carry
# exactly what two 3s carry. That is why the zip costs you a high self and not a
# club - this pattern holds 18, the same as the all-3s version.
#
# The 5 is a triple-height self, peaking about 3.4 m at this tempo. It is the
# real price of a travelling zip, and it is worth knowing before you start.
#
#
# WHY THIS IS 18 AND NOT 17
#
# See star-17c-one-zip.txt for the 17-club version. There the zip does NOT
# travel - E zips on every single one of their self beats, permanently.
#
# That is forced, not a choice. Over ten beats a juggler's five selfs can only
# ever total 5, 15 or 25; nothing else is throwable. Fifteen is the ordinary
# case (five 3s, or any mix like 5-1-3-3-3 here). Getting to 17 clubs needs one
# juggler down at a total of 5 - and the only five selfs that add to 5 are five
# zips. So at 17 clubs somebody zips every time, and the job cannot move. Making
# the period longer does not help; the same arithmetic comes back.
#
# So: 17 clubs with one permanent zipper, or 18 clubs with the zip going round.
#
# Passing is across the star:  A->C  B->D  C->E  D->A  E->B
# E still stands half a beat off the others, which is what lets all five of you
# throw doubles; C's and E's passes are the 4.5s.

name: 18c star travelling zip David
beat: 0.30

jugglers:
  A at 0deg
  B at 72deg
  C at 144deg start_hand L
  D at 216deg start_hand L
  E at 288deg phase 0.5

start:
  A: R=2 L=2
  B: R=2 L=2
  C: R=1 L=2
  D: R=1 L=2
  E: R=2 L=2

loop:
  A: 4pC line 5   4pC line 1   4pC line 3   4pC line 3   4pC line 3
  B: 4pD line 3   4pD line 5   4pD line 1   4pD line 3   4pD line 3
  C: 3   4.5pE line   3   4.5pE line   5   4.5pE line   1   4.5pE line   3   4.5pE line
  D: 3   4pA line     3   4pA line     3   4pA line     5   4pA line     1   4pA line
  E: 4.5pB line 1 4.5pB line 3 4.5pB line 3 4.5pB line 3 4.5pB line 5
