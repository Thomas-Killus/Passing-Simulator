# 17 clubs AND 2-count: everybody passes from the right hand on every other beat.
#
# The catch is that you cannot all throw the same thing. With five people doing
# an identical 2-beat loop the club count is forced to a multiple of 5 - 15 or 20,
# never 17 (see the note at the bottom). So the five of you take three different
# jobs:
#
#     A     single pass, self          3 clubs
#     B, C  DOUBLE pass, self          3 clubs
#     D, E  single pass, HEFF          4 clubs
#
# Everyone still passes from the right hand, on the same beats, across the star.
# B and C throw the heavy pass; D and E throw the heavy self. A is the light one.
#
# Passing:  A->C  B->D  C->E  D->A  E->B
#
# Why not all the same? Club count = 5 x your average throw height. A 2-beat loop
# has an average that is a whole or half number, so five identical jugglers can
# only ever total a multiple of 5. Getting 17 out of five identical people needs a
# loop 5 beats long - which is star-17c.txt, and a 5-beat loop makes your passes
# alternate hands. So: 17 clubs, right-hand-only passing, everyone identical -
# pick two of the three.

name: 17c star 2-count
beat: 0.33

jugglers:
  A at 0deg
  B at 72deg
  C at 144deg
  D at 216deg
  E at 288deg

start:
  A: R=2 L=1
  B: R=2 L=1
  C: R=2 L=1
  D: R=2 L=2
  E: R=2 L=2

loop:
  A: 3pC 3
  B: 4pD 3
  C: 4pE 3
  D: 3pA 4
  E: 3pB 4
