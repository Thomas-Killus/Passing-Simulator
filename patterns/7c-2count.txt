# 7 clubs, 2 people, 2-count with double passes thrown LINE - right hand to your
# partner's LEFT hand. This is how most people throw it.
#
# A holds 4 clubs, 2 in each hand, and opens with the double pass from the right.
# B holds 3, two left and one right, and opens with a SELF from the left hand,
# then throws their double pass from the right one beat later. After that each of
# them passes every other beat, always from the right hand, always line.
#
# WHAT MAKES IT LINE
#
# B's hands are offset one beat from A's: when A throws right, B throws left.
# That is the `start_hand L` on B's line below, and it is the whole trick. Which
# hand catches a pass is fixed by how long the club is in the air AND by whether
# the two of you alternate hands in step:
#
#     hands in step        4 -> partner's right (cross),  3 -> their left (line)
#     hands offset by one  4 -> partner's LEFT  (line),   3 -> their right (cross)
#
# Spin has nothing to do with it - you can put two spins on any of these. It only
# changes how the club looks in the air, not where it lands.
#
# The `line` marks on the passes below are checked, not obeyed. The engine works
# out where each club really lands and complains if the file disagrees, so you
# cannot quietly write down something unthrowable.

name: 7c 2-count
beat: 0.32

jugglers:
  A at (0, 1.75)
  B at (0, -1.75) start_hand L

start:
  A: R=2 L=2
  B: R=1 L=2

loop:
  A: 4pB line 3
  B: 3 4pA line
